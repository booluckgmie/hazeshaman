## How impact is computed
* A state's closure chance is the chance of its **highest-risk station**; a state is *at risk* when that chance is at or above the cost-optimal trigger.
* **If schools close** = all schools, students and teachers in the state (what a state-wide order would cover).
* **Likely affected tomorrow** = closure chance x schools in the state; for Malaysia it is the sum of that over all 16 states, metric by metric.
* Closures are in practice often **district-level** (see note 6), so state-wide totals are an upper bound for partial orders.
