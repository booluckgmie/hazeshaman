# 2. The model and how it was tested

## What is predicted
For every **station** and every hour **H = 12:00 ... 23:00** (so each new hourly report refreshes every station), using only what is known at H:

* inputs: ln API at 8 AM, noon, H-3 and H; ln yesterday's daily mean; the streak of prior days with mean > 150; season (sin/cos of day of year); plus a state effect shared by its stations;
* targets for tomorrow (logistic regressions): 7 AM API > 100, 7 AM API > 150, any reading > 180 during 06:00-14:00, daily mean > 150;
* tomorrow's 7 AM level: ridge on ln API with a Gaussian residual, which gives the 10-90% fan in the trend chart.

## Closure risk
KPM closes schools when **API > 180**, or **API > 150 for three consecutive days**. The model mirrors both triggers:

`closure risk = P(>180) + (1 - P(>180)) x [at least 2 prior days > 150] x P(daily mean > 150)`

The three dashboard scenarios follow from it: **Best** = P(7 AM <= 100) x (1 - closure); **Worst** = closure; **Base** = the remainder.
Inputs beyond about 4 standard deviations are clamped and flagged, so What-if cannot extrapolate silently.

## How it was tested
Trained on data before **2025-07-01**; tested on the days after it, which the model never saw. For the closure target:

| Decision hour | Station-days | Closure days | Brier | Always-base-rate Brier | Brier skill | AUC (all days) | AUC (days already > 100) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 PM | 30,025 | 416 | 0.0047 | 0.0139 | 65.6% | 0.998 | 0.931 |
| 4 PM | 30,025 | 416 | 0.0045 | 0.0139 | 67.2% | 0.998 | 0.935 |
| 5 PM | 30,024 | 416 | 0.0045 | 0.0139 | 66.9% | 0.998 | 0.934 |
| 6 PM | 30,024 | 416 | 0.0047 | 0.0139 | 65.6% | 0.998 | 0.940 |
| 7 PM | 30,024 | 416 | 0.0043 | 0.0139 | 68.4% | 0.998 | 0.949 |
| 8 PM | 30,022 | 415 | 0.0041 | 0.0138 | 70.1% | 0.998 | 0.951 |
| 9 PM | 30,022 | 415 | 0.0038 | 0.0138 | 72.1% | 0.999 | 0.956 |

* **Brier skill** compares the model with always predicting the base rate (about 1.4% of station-days reach the closure trigger). It rises from 66% at 3 PM to 72% at 9 PM.
* The headline **AUC on all days (about 0.998)** is flattered by the many easy clear-air days. The honest number is the last column, the ranking quality on days that were already unhealthy (above 100) at the decision hour: 0.93 to 0.96. The dashboard shows the harder numbers first.
* At 6 PM the 7 AM > 100 target has AUC 0.990 and the 7 AM level has an RMSE of 0.18 in ln units.

## Limits
* The hold-out is one continuous period, dominated by a few haze episodes; the skill figures are not a promise for a different year.
* Sabah, Sarawak, Kelantan and Labuan have far less history, so their models lean on the shared structure and the state effect.
* The model forecasts DOE readings, not health outcomes, and not the decisions authorities will actually take (see note 6).
