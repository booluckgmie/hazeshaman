# 1. Data sources, cleaning and known gaps

## The problem: nobody publishes a haze history
The DOE hourly API (`https://eqms.doe.gov.my/api3/publicportalapims/apitablehourly`) returns only the **last 24 hours** per station
(68 stations x 24 h); the older APIMS feed and the website expose "yesterday" and "today" only. There is no official archive or bulk download,
so a forecaster, an audit of past closure decisions or a seasonality study can only use history that somebody recorded. Three consequences:

1. Every probability in Hazeshaman depends on history collected by others (`booluckgmie/sharecode`) and by this repository's own hourly job.
2. **Gaps are permanent.** There are no readings between 2024-11-04 and 2025-06-30, and Sabah, Sarawak, Kelantan and Labuan only appear from 2023-09.
3. **Naive collectors corrupt the record.** The 24 h window spans two calendar days; the early collector stamped the whole window with one date, so hours
   that "had not been reached yet" carried yesterday's values under today's date. Those values must be discarded.

## Bad-value guard (applied to every source, and again in the browser)
* hours stamped **after the current Malaysia hour** are dropped;
* non-numeric flags (`&`, `*`, `c`, `-`, blanks), off-the-hour stamps (e.g. `05:15AM`) and values outside 1-500 are dropped;
* no row may be dated after its own file's day;
* a daily mean needs at least 18 hourly readings; gaps of up to 3 h are interpolated, longer gaps stay missing.
